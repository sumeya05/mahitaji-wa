Mahitaji Enterprises Limited

Three Decades of Distribution Excellence

Founded on the principles of reliability and efficiency, Mahitaji Enterprises Limited has evolved from a local startup into Kenya's premier FMCG distribution powerhouse. Based in the heart of the Baba Dogo Industrial Park, we act as the vital link between global manufacturers and the local retail landscape.

Our Operational Strength

We don't just move products; we manage a complex supply chain designed for speed. With a 100,000 sq. ft. modern warehouse and a dedicated fleet of over 50 delivery trucks, we maintain a seamless flow of goods. This infrastructure allows us to serve over 3,000 active clients, ensuring that store shelves remain stocked and communities have access to essentials.

Quality & Compliance

Every product that passes through our facility meets the highest standards of safety. We work exclusively with KEBS-certified brands, ensuring that from staple foods to personal care items, our partners and consumers receive nothing but the best. Our commitment to competitive pricing and professional service has made us a trusted household name across the region.

"Our strategic hub in Baba Dogo allows us to provide lightning-fast fulfillment to our retail partners. We take pride in being more than just a distributor—we are a growth partner for the Kenyan economy."

— The Executive Board

Our Vision

To be the most preferred and reliable FMCG distribution partner in East Africa, recognized for our efficiency, integrity, and contribution to household well-being.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between manufacturers and consumers by providing seamless logistics, quality assurance, and competitive pricing through our extensive retail network.
