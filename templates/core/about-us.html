Providing the BestFMCG Solutions

Mahitaji Enterprises Limited is a premier FMCG distributor dedicated to connecting world-class manufacturers with the Kenyan retail market. We have grown from a local distributor into a market leader, operating from our strategic hub in Baba Dogo Industrial Park to serve over 3,000 active clients across the region.

Our strength lies in our extensive distribution network and our commitment to reliability. With a robust fleet of over 50 delivery trucks and a 100,000 sq. ft. warehouse facility, we ensure that essential products—from staple foods and beverages to detergents and personal care items—reach our retail partners and households efficiently and on time.

For nearly three decades, our customers have trusted us because of our unwavering focus on quality and competitive pricing. We take pride in being the bridge that brings high-quality, KEBS-certified products to the market, helping businesses grow and families thrive through consistent supply and professional service.
