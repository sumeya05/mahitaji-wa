Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?
Log into your account and visit the 'My Orders' section in your Dashboard for real-time tracking updates once your items have been dispatched.
What is the minimum order for wholesalers?
Minimum wholesale orders vary by product category but generally start at KSh 50,000 to qualify for bulk pricing tiers.
Do you deliver outside Nairobi?
Yes, we deliver across all major towns in Kenya. Orders outside the Nairobi Metropolitan area are typically fulfilled within 2-3 business days.
How can I apply for a credit line?
Established retailers with a consistent purchase history of over 3 months can apply for credit terms. Please contact your account manager or email finance@mahitaji.co.ke for details.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept M-Pesa (Express and Till), Bank Transfers, and major Credit/Debit cards. Cash on delivery is available for specific verified routes.
