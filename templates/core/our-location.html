Visiting Our Facility

To maintain the highest standards of safety and operational efficiency within **Prabhaki Industrial Park**, we kindly request all visitors, retailers, and partners to observe our site protocols.

Identification

All visitors must present a valid national ID or passport at the main security gate for entry clearance.

Safety First

Safety is paramount. Please stick to designated pedestrian zones and follow warehouse floor markings.

Check-in

Upon arrival at our warehouse unit, please sign in at the reception desk before proceeding to loading bays.

Loading

Drivers must remain with their vehicles during the loading process unless instructed otherwise by staff.
