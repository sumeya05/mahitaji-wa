Our Corporate Philosophy

Established in 1997, Mahitaji Enterprises Limited serves as the essential bridge connecting world-class manufacturers with over 3,000 active retail clients. Operating from our hub in Baba Dogo Industrial Park, we ensure the seamless movement of quality FMCG goods to every Kenyan household.

Our Vision

To be the most preferred and reliable FMCG distribution partner in East Africa, recognized for our efficiency, integrity, and unwavering contribution to household well-being.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between manufacturers and consumers by providing seamless logistics, rigorous quality assurance, and competitive pricing through our extensive retail network.

The Mahitaji Commitment

Operating from our strategic hub in Baba Dogo Industrial Park, we manage a dedicated fleet of 10+ specialized delivery vehicles and a 100,000 sq. ft. warehouse facility. Our commitment is to ensure that essential products reach our 3,000+ active clients efficiently and on time.

Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make at Mahitaji Enterprises Limited.

Integrity

Transparent dealings and strong moral principles with all partners.

Customer Focus

We grow when our clients grow through reliable service and pricing.

Quality Assurance

KEBS-certified products ensuring safety and trust.

Reliability

Consistent supply backed by strong logistics infrastructure.

Community & Growth

At Mahitaji Enterprises Limited, we recognize that our role extends beyond distribution. We are committed to the economic growth of Kenya by:

  • Supporting Local Retail: Empowering small and medium-sized shops with reliable inventory.
  • Ensuring Food Security: Maintaining a steady supply of staple goods across the region.
  • Professional Standards: Leading the industry with professional-grade logistics and ethical operations.
