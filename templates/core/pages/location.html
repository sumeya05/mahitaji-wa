Visiting our facility

Site protocols for visitors and partners

To maintain the highest standards of safety and operational efficiency within Prabhaki Industrial Park, we kindly request all visitors, retailers and partners to observe our site protocols.

Identification

All visitors must present a valid national ID or passport at the main security gate for entry clearance.

Safety first

Stick to designated pedestrian zones and follow warehouse floor markings at all times.

Check-in

Please sign in at reception before proceeding to the loading bays upon arrival.

Loading

Drivers must remain with their vehicles during loading unless instructed otherwise by our team.

Plan your visit

Collecting an order or dropping in to register?

Bring your business permit and KRA PIN for on-the-spot registration, or call ahead so we can have your order ready at the bay.
