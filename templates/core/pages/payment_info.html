Payment policies

Choose the way that suits your business

At Mahitaji, we provide flexible and secure payment options tailored for both individual retailers and large-scale wholesalers.

M-Pesa STK push

Fastest

Enjoy faster processing with our secure online gateway. We support M-Pesa Express (STK push) so you never have to type a paybill number.

  1. Choose M-Pesa at checkout and confirm the phone number.
  2. A payment prompt appears on your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN.
  3. Your order is confirmed instantly and dispatched on the next route.

Cash on delivery

Verified routes

Available for verified business accounts on our regular Nairobi delivery routes. Pay the driver on arrival by cash, M-Pesa Buy Goods Till or business cheque.

  • Payment must be settled in full at the point of delivery.
  • Available once your account and delivery address are verified.
  • Goods are released only after payment is confirmed.

Bank transfer / cheque

Account customers

For wholesale and account customers placing larger orders. Transfer to our bank account using your order number as the reference, or settle by business cheque on delivery.

  • Bank details are printed on your pro-forma invoice.
  • Orders are dispatched once funds are cleared.
  • Credit terms are available to established partners after assessment.

Security guarantee

All transactions are encrypted and processed through industry-standard secure protocols. We do not store your M-Pesa PIN or full payment details on our servers.

  • M-Pesa payments are confirmed directly by Safaricom.
  • Every order receives an itemised, VAT-compliant invoice.
  • Our drivers never ask for your PIN or card details.
