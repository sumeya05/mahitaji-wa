Goods return policy

Our standard return procedure

We take pride in the quality of goods we supply to our partners. However, if you receive a damaged, expired or incorrect item, please follow our standard return procedure.

  • 48-hour window

    Returns must be initiated within 48 hours of delivery. Beyond this window, we may be unable to verify the claim with our suppliers.

  • Original condition

    Items must be in their original packaging, unopened and unused. Labels and seals must remain intact.

  • Perishables & fresh goods

    Fresh or perishable goods must be inspected at the point of delivery. Returns for these specific items are only accepted at the moment of offloading.

  • Refunds & credits

    Once a return is approved, refunds are processed to your original payment method or credited to your Mahitaji account within 5–7 business days.

How to start a return

Three simple steps

Document the issue

Take clear photos of the item, its packaging and the delivery note within 48 hours of receiving your order.

Contact claims

Email claims@mahitaji.co.ke or call us with your order number, the affected items and your photos. We acknowledge every claim within one business day.

Collection & resolution

Our driver collects the goods on the next scheduled route. Once approved, we replace the item or issue a refund or account credit.
