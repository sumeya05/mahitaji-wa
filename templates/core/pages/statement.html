Our corporate philosophy

The essential bridge between manufacturers and Kenyan households

Established in 1997, Mahitaji Enterprises Limited serves as the essential bridge connecting world-class manufacturers with over 3,000 active retail clients.

Operating from our hub in Baba Dogo Industrial Park, we ensure the seamless movement of quality FMCG goods to every Kenyan household. Our role is simple to state and demanding to deliver: the right product, in the right condition, at the right shop, on time, every time.

Our vision

To be the most preferred and reliable FMCG distribution partner in East Africa, recognised for our efficiency, integrity and unwavering contribution to household well-being.

Our mission

To bridge the gap between manufacturers and consumers by providing seamless logistics, rigorous quality assurance and competitive pricing through our extensive retail network.

The Mahitaji commitment

Essential products, delivered efficiently and on time

Operating from our strategic hub in Baba Dogo Industrial Park, we manage a dedicated fleet of 50+ specialised delivery vehicles and a 100,000 sq ft warehouse facility. Our commitment is to ensure that essential products reach our 3,000+ active clients efficiently and on time.
