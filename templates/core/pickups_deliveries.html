Pickups & Deliveries

Standard Delivery

We deliver within Nairobi and its environs within 24 hours of order confirmation. For regions outside Nairobi, delivery typically takes 2-3 business days depending on the specific location.

Nairobi, Kenya

Working Hours: Monday — Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a notification via SMS or Email with the contact details of the delivery personnel assigned to your package.
