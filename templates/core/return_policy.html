Goods Return Policy

We take pride in the quality of goods we supply to our partners. However, if you receive a damaged, expired, or incorrect item, please follow our standard return procedure:

  • 48-Hour Window Returns must be initiated within 48 hours of delivery. Beyond this window, we may be unable to verify the claim with our suppliers.
  • Original Condition Items must be in their original packaging, unopened, and unused. Labels and seals must remain intact.
  • Perishables & Fresh Goods Fresh or perishable goods must be inspected at the point of delivery. Returns for these specific items are only accepted at the moment of offloading.
  • Refunds & Credits Once a return is approved, refunds are processed to your original payment method or credited to your Mahitaji account within 5-7 business days.

Need help with a return?
Contact our claims department immediately at claims@mahitaji.co.ke or call +254 750 530 130.
